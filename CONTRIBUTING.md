# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the **ITS-LMA Review Living Evidence Repository**.

We welcome suggestions that help keep the literature map accurate, current, and useful. Contributions may include newly published studies, updated publication versions, corrected bibliographic information, public code or project links, and other resources relevant to the scope of the review.

---

## What You Can Suggest

Contributions are particularly welcome for:

- newly published or newly available ITS foundation-model studies;
- archival versions of studies currently represented by preprints;
- corrections to paper titles, authors, venues, years, or links;
- official project pages or public code repositories;
- datasets, benchmarks, simulators, or evaluation resources;
- relevant review or survey articles; and
- corrections to study-family or source-version information.

---

## Scope

A suggested primary study should involve a foundation model with a substantive transportation-specific role, such as:

- multimodal evidence interpretation or grounding;
- semantic reasoning;
- evidence retrieval or organisation;
- specialist-tool orchestration;
- transportation decision support or planning;
- simulation or scenario generation;
- bounded transportation actions; or
- outcome-responsive decision making.

Studies that use conventional machine learning, optimisation, simulation, or control without a substantive foundation-model component may still be useful as contextual resources, but they are not automatically eligible as primary study families.

---

## How to Suggest a Study

You may submit a suggestion through a GitHub **Issue** or **Pull Request**.

Please provide, where available:

- **Title**
- **Authors**
- **Year**
- **Venue or publication status**
- **DOI or official paper link**
- **Project page**
- **Official code repository**
- **Brief reason for relevance to the review**
- **Suggested application domain**

The five application domains used in this repository are:

1. Prediction and Network Understanding
2. Autonomous Driving and Vehicle Interaction
3. Signal Control and Public Operations
4. Traffic Safety and Incident Management
5. Planning, Simulation, and Human–Machine Collaboration

---

## Review of Contributions

Suggested records are checked before being incorporated into the curated evidence map.

The maintainers verify, as applicable:

- relevance to the repository scope;
- publication and source version;
- whether the record represents a new study family or an update to an existing family;
- application-domain assignment;
- availability of official paper, project, code, or data links; and
- consistency with the evidence framework used in the review.

Submission of a study does not guarantee inclusion as a primary study family.

---

## Frozen Manuscript Corpus

The manuscript evidence map is frozen through **3 August 2026** and contains **42 primary study families**.

The frozen corpus is preserved as the evidence base underlying the quantitative results reported in the manuscript.

Newly identified literature after the cutoff is therefore added to the **post-cutoff living evidence record** rather than silently incorporated into the frozen manuscript counts.

Corrections to the frozen evidence map are made only when supported by a documented source, eligibility, version-resolution, or coding correction.

---

## Evidence Classifications

The C0–C3 capability, E0–E4 validation, P1–P3 proposition, and Q1–Q8 methodological-concern classifications are assigned by the review team using the documented evidence framework.

Contributors are welcome to point out evidence that may support a correction, but proposed classifications are independently checked before any repository change is made.

See [`taxonomy.md`](taxonomy.md) for the definitions used in this repository.

---

## Corrections

If you identify an error in an existing record, please provide:

1. the affected study or repository entry;
2. the information that appears to be incorrect;
3. the proposed correction; and
4. an official publication, repository, dataset, or other source supporting the correction.

Substantive corrections and literature updates are documented in [`UPDATE_LOG.md`](UPDATE_LOG.md).

---

## Questions

For questions about the repository, literature coverage, or a possible contribution, please open a GitHub Issue.
