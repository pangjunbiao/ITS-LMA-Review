## Update Policy

A literature-update check will be conducted **at least once every two months until the survey is accepted**.

Each update entry will record, where applicable:

- date of the literature check;
- newly identified candidate studies;
- newly eligible study families;
- publication-version updates;
- study-family consolidation or correction;
- relevant new surveys, datasets, benchmarks, or resources;
- exclusions that materially affect interpretation; and
- any effect on the living evidence map.

Post-cutoff additions remain distinct from the manuscript's frozen evidence corpus unless a documented manuscript-level update is performed.
