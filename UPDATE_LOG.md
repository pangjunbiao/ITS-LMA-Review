## Update Policy

A literature-update check will be conducted **at least once every two months until the survey is accepted**.

Each completed update will record, where applicable:

- the date of the literature check;
- newly identified candidate studies;
- newly eligible post-cutoff study families;
- publication-version updates;
- study-family consolidation or correction;
- relevant new surveys, datasets, benchmarks, simulators, or other resources;
- exclusions or screening decisions that materially affect interpretation; and
- any resulting change to the living evidence record.

A completed literature check will also be logged when **no new eligible study family is identified**, so that the maintenance history remains transparent.

Post-cutoff additions remain separate from the manuscript's frozen evidence corpus unless a documented manuscript-level update is performed.

Corrections to the frozen corpus will be recorded explicitly rather than silently changing the historical evidence map.
