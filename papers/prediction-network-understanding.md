### Evidence Summary

UrbanGPT is the only primary study family in the frozen corpus whose principal evaluated role is **prediction and network understanding**.

Within the review framework, UrbanGPT is classified as:

- **C0** — pre-agentic transportation-specific capability;
- **E1** — offline evaluation;
- **P1-P** — partial evidence for behaviour and facility semantics;
- **P2-N** — no proposition-specific evaluation of evidence reconciliation; and
- **P3-D** — direct evidence for multidimensional transportation integration.

Its evaluated contribution is a spatio-temporal foundation-model approach to urban prediction. The evidence supports the feasibility of foundation-model-based spatio-temporal integration in this study family, but does not by itself establish domain-wide forecasting superiority, operational decision benefit, or deployment readiness.

For the full family-level taxonomy, methodological-concern profile, and principal limitation, see the [frozen evidence map](../data/frozen_evidence_map_2026-08-03.csv).
