# Post-Cutoff Literature Updates

This page tracks relevant literature identified **after the manuscript evidence cutoff of 3 August 2026**.

The manuscript itself is based on a frozen corpus of **42 primary study families**. Post-cutoff records are maintained separately so that the living repository can remain current without changing the evidence counts reported in the manuscript.

---

## How Post-Cutoff Records Are Handled

A newly identified record is checked to determine whether it is:

- a **new eligible primary study family**;
- a **new publication version of an existing study family**;
- a **relevant contextual study or resource**;
- a **new survey, dataset, benchmark, or simulator**; or
- **outside the scope** of the review.

New records are not automatically added to the frozen manuscript corpus.

Where appropriate, eligible post-cutoff studies may be assigned the same application-domain and evidence-framework descriptors used in the review.

See [`taxonomy.md`](taxonomy.md) for the evidence framework and [`UPDATE_LOG.md`](UPDATE_LOG.md) for dated repository updates.
