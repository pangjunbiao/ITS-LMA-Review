# Related Surveys

This page lists the review and survey articles used to position the contribution of **“Large Multimodal Agents for Intelligent Transportation Systems: Architectures, Evidence, and Deployment Challenges.”**

The surveys are grouped for navigation only. Individual studies differ in scope, methodology, terminology, and analytical emphasis.

## Position of the Present Review

The present review complements these surveys by jointly linking:

- model-level, system-level, and hybrid multimodality;
- system architecture and specialist-tool boundaries;
- C0–C3 demonstrated capability;
- E0–E4 validation setting;
- P1–P3 proposition-specific evidence;
- Q1–Q8 methodological concerns; and
- action authority and deployment boundaries.

The comparison is intended to clarify analytical scope rather than rank previous surveys.
